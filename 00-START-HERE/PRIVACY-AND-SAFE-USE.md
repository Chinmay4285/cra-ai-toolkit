# Privacy & Safe Use Guidelines

**For Clinical Research Associates using the CRA AI Copilot**

---

## 🔒 Before You Start

This toolkit helps you learn about studies and prepare for monitoring visits. But you must follow your organization's policies for using AI tools.

**Before uploading ANY study documents to Claude, ask yourself:**

1. ✅ **Does my organization permit using Claude for this?**
   - Check your company's AI usage policy
   - Ask your supervisor if unsure
   - Some organizations have approved AI tools; others restrict them

2. ✅ **Am I authorized to upload these specific documents?**
   - Protocol: Usually OK
   - Patient data: Usually NOT OK
   - Site information: Usually OK, but may need de-identification
   - Confidential drug information: Check first

3. ✅ **Does this need de-identification?**
   - Patient names: Don't include
   - Patient medical record numbers: Don't include
   - Specific site locations: Consider de-identifying (use "Site ABC" instead of "University Medical Center")
   - Your name/contact info: OK to include if needed

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT LIMITS

### What Claude Conversations Are NOT Private
- Claude may review conversations for safety and improvement
- Your conversation data goes to Anthropic's servers
- Don't assume your conversation is confidential
- Follow your company's data protection and privacy policies

### What NOT to Upload
- 🚫 Individual patient medical records or charts
- 🚫 Patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers
- 🚫 Actual study enrollment data with patient details
- 🚫 Confidential business information not meant for AI review
- 🚫 Secrets, API keys, passwords, or credentials
- 🚫 Information restricted by law (e.g., genetic data, psychiatric records)

### What's Generally Safe to Upload
- ✅ Study protocol (without patient identifiers)
- ✅ Protocol amendments and SAP
- ✅ Monitoring plan and study procedures
- ✅ Informed Consent Form
- ✅ Study investigator's brochure
- ✅ CRF instructions and study manuals
- ✅ General study procedures (without patient data)

## 🛡️ BEST PRACTICES FOR SAFE USE

### 1. De-Identify When Possible
Instead of: `Study at Massachusetts General Hospital`  
Use: `Study at Site ABC`

Instead of: `Dr. Smith's monitoring visit notes`  
Use: `Monitoring visit notes`

### 2. Share Carefully
- The Claude Project link is private to you
- Don't share Project links with colleagues unless authorized
- If sharing study information, share the reference cards/cheat sheets, not the actual Project conversation

### 3. Follow Company Policies
- Check if your company requires approval before using AI tools
- Some organizations require signing agreements before using Claude
- Some have designated "approved AI tools" — confirm Claude is on the list

### 4. Report Breaches
If you accidentally upload something you shouldn't:
- Tell your supervisor immediately
- Document what was uploaded and when
- Claude support can sometimes remove specific conversations

### 5. Use for Learning, Not Decisions Alone
This AI Copilot is a thinking partner, not a decision-maker:
- Use Claude's suggestions to inform your judgment
- Always verify against approved study documents
- Consult with supervisors on major decisions
- Don't rely solely on Claude for protocol interpretation

### 6. Verify Study-Specific Answers
Even though Claude is sourced to your documents:
- Always double-check important answers against the protocol
- Flag inconsistencies you find
- Don't assume Claude's answer is correct without verification
- Escalate to sponsor for ambiguous protocol language

## 🏥 COMPLIANCE REMINDERS

### Study Sponsor Requirements
- Your sponsor may have specific policies on AI use
- Some studies restrict document sharing with external tools
- When in doubt, ask your Study Manager or Medical Monitor

### Regulatory Compliance
- Ensure your use follows 21 CFR Part 11 (if applicable)
- For FDA-regulated studies, ensure adequate documentation
- For international studies, follow regional privacy laws (GDPR, etc.)

### Site Agreements
- If you're the CRA at a clinical site, follow site agreements
- Sites may have restrictions on document sharing
- Ensure your use doesn't violate site data use agreements

### Data Protection
- For studies with European participants: GDPR compliance
- For studies with Canadian participants: PIPEDA compliance
- For healthcare data: HIPAA/HIPAA-equivalent compliance
- Keep study data secure; use Claude only as intended

## ❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

### Q: Can I upload a redacted patient chart?
**A:** Usually not necessary and not recommended. You don't need individual patient charts to understand study procedures. If you need to understand how to assess a specific patient scenario, describe the scenario in general terms rather than uploading actual charts.

### Q: Can I tell Claude about specific patients at my site?
**A:** Describe situations in general terms ("a 65-year-old with elevated baseline lab") rather than identifying patients. Never include patient names or identifiers.

### Q: What if I accidentally included something sensitive?
**A:** 
1. Tell your supervisor immediately
2. Note what was uploaded and when
3. You can delete the conversation from Claude
4. Contact Claude support if needed to request conversation removal
5. Document the incident per your organization's requirements

### Q: Can I print cheat sheets Claude creates?
**A:** Yes! The reference cards Claude creates (one-pagers, quick reviews, skill menus) are great to print and keep at your desk. They don't contain sensitive data and are derived from the study protocol.

### Q: Can I share Claude's explanations with colleagues?
**A:** Yes, you can share the learning content Claude creates (explanations, diagrams, flashcards). Don't share the actual Claude conversation link unless authorized.

### Q: Is Claude's answer always correct about my study?
**A:** No. Claude is helpful, but it can make mistakes. Always verify study-specific answers against the approved protocol and study documents. If you find an error, tell Claude and your supervisor.

## 🚨 RED FLAGS — Stop and Ask Before Proceeding

Stop and get approval from your supervisor if:

- 🛑 A document is marked "Confidential — Internal Use Only"
- 🛑 A document contains specific patient data (even de-identified)
- 🛑 You're unsure if a document is approved for external tools
- 🛑 Your organization hasn't given written approval for Claude use
- 🛑 A document contains genetic data, psychiatric information, or other highly sensitive data
- 🛑 You're asked to do something that feels ethically wrong
- 🛑 A study sponsor explicitly restricted AI tool use

**When in doubt, ask. It's always better to get approval first than to apologize later.**
