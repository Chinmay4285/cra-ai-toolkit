## HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE

1. **Fill it out** — Complete the sections that are relevant to your study. You don't need to fill in everything.

2. **Save it** — Keep a copy of this filled form for future conversations.

3. **Paste it into Claude** — Copy all of this (including headers) and paste it as your first message in Claude.

4. **Update it** — As you learn more about the study, update this context and paste the new version.

5. **Use it for multiple conversations** — The same filled context can be pasted into multiple Claude conversations if you need to start fresh or work on different topics.

---

## PRIVACY NOTE

**Only include information your organization permits you to share with AI tools.**

- ☐ Study names and numbers are typically OK to share
- ⚠️ Avoid: Patient names, initials, patient identifiers, specific enrollment details that could identify patients
- ⚠️ Site names may need de-identification (use "Site ABC" instead of "University Medical Center")
- ⚠️ Check your company's AI usage policy before uploading sensitive information
