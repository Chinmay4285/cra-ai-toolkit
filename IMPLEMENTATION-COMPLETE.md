## How to Use This Toolkit

### First Time Setup (30 minutes)

1. **Read the foundation** (10 min)
   - README.md
   - QUICK-START.md

2. **Create your study context** (10 min)
   - Fill out study-context-template.md

3. **Start the Study Brain** (10 min)
   - Load study-expert.md with your protocol
   - This is your central hub for everything

### Daily Use

- Keep the Study Brain conversation open
- Ask questions and use commands
- Refer to specialized prompts as needed
- Study and practice using learning prompts

### Before Monitoring Visits

- Use monitoring-visit-prep.md
- Review critical facts (rapid-review.md)
- Practice scenarios (scenario-simulator.md)
- Run risk assessment (risk-based-review.md)

## Best Practices for Users

### DO:
✅ Start with Study Brain
✅ Keep one conversation going per study
✅ Provide full study context upfront
✅ Verify important information against protocol
✅ Use specialized prompts as needed
✅ Practice with scenarios before monitoring
✅ Review weak areas regularly
✅ Adapt prompts to your situation

### DON'T:
❌ Start new conversations each time
❌ Expect Claude to be an authority (it's a thinking tool)
❌ Skip source verification on critical info
❌ Assume generic guidance applies to your study
❌ Make decisions based solely on Claude output
❌ Upload data you're unsure about sharing
❌ Treat this as replacement for protocol
❌ Skip the source-grounding rules

## Toolkit Philosophy

**This toolkit reflects the belief that:**

1. CRAs need better tools for complex study knowledge
2. AI can augment CRA judgment, not replace it
3. Learning works better through multiple modalities
4. Source grounding prevents dangerous hallucinations
5. Practice and feedback build expertise faster
6. Visual learning, stories, and scenarios stick better
7. CRAs' time is valuable—tools should minimize busywork
8. Professional CRA work requires professional tools
